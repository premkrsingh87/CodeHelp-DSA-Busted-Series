How it works

Script in, film out.

Pipeline
1 · Write — paste narration or a prompt list and press Build shot list. Each shot gets a prompt, a caption and a duration paced off its word count.
2 · Look — pick a Style DNA. Its suffix rides on every prompt so the film holds one visual language; turn on continuity to chain each frame as reference for the next.
3 · Generate — price it, then fire. Shots queue in parallel and poll until done; the button turns into a stop control while the batch runs. Anything you have already generated stays in Task history (J) to reuse or delete.
4 · Time — drag clips to reorder, drag their right edge to retime, split at the playhead, set motion and cuts per shot. Drop in a voiceover and fit the film to it, then lay a music bed underneath — it loops to fill and ducks under the voice.
5 · Export — the canvas renders with motion, transitions, captions and grade, and records to a video file with your audio mixed in. Every edit is undoable.
